@bpcarter I looked at the 2.3.0.1 code (`v2.3.0.1_stable_2026-04-24`), and I think two things explain what you see.

1. **The free space is checked too late.** In 2.3.0.1 the restore cache has no size limit by default. It removes cached volumes when the free space in the temporary folder drops below `--restore-volume-cache-min-free` (1 GB by default). But it checks only when a volume has been decrypted and is added to the cache. Several volumes are downloaded and decrypted at the same time, and each one needs about twice its size in the temporary folder while it is decrypted. They can fill the disk before the next check, which matches the `CryptographicException: There is not enough space on the disk` in your log. This part is still the same on master; #7780 makes room before each download.
2. **Files from failed attempts are not removed.** In 2.3.0.1 the restore does not remove the cached volumes when it ends or fails, so the files of each failed attempt can stay in `%TEMP%` (Duplicati removes `dup-*` files older than 30 days on its own). This is fixed in 2.4.0.0 and later.

Until then, these should help:

- Close Duplicati and delete the old `dup-*` files in `C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\Temp`.
- Update to 2.4.1.0 (stable), which removes the cached volumes when a restore ends or fails.
- Add `--restore-legacy=true` to the restore options. The legacy restore works on one volume at a time and needs much less temporary space.
- Or point `--tempdir` to a drive with more free space, or set `--restore-volume-cache-hint` (for example `4GB`) to limit the cache, and lower `--restore-volume-downloaders` and `--restore-volume-decryptors`.
